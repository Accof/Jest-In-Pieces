Anatomy meshes in art/anatomy (head.glb, neck.glb, chest.glb, belly.glb,
pelvis.glb, joints.glb) are built from two CC BY 4.0 sources by the scripts in
art/anatomy/source/ (joined, welded, decimated, cut and fitted to each body).
They are derived works under the same terms; the attribution below must ship
with the game (credits screen or bundled notice).

1. BodyParts3D
   BodyParts3D, (c) The Database Center for Life Science, licensed under
   CC Attribution 4.0 International (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
   https://dbarchive.biosciencedbc.jp/en/bodyparts3d/
   Used: skull, mandible, teeth, brain, vertebrae, ribs, sternum, costal
   cartilage, clavicles, intervertebral disks, hip bones, sacrum, stomach,
   colon, kidneys, muscles of the trunk, oesophagus; humerus, radius, ulna,
   femur, tibia and fibula (joints.glb).
   Read via the human-atlas packing (github.com/ashemag/human-atlas).

2. HuBMAP CCF 3D Reference Object Library (Human Reference Atlas)
   Licensed under CC BY 4.0. Visible Human male models by Kristen Browne
   and Heidi Schlehlein. https://github.com/hubmapconsortium/ccf-releases (v1.3)
   Used: heart (VH_M_Heart), lungs (VH_M_Lung), liver (VH_M_Liver),
   small intestine (VH_M_Small_Intestine).

Changes made: geometry merged per region, decimated, smoothed, re-scaled and
placed on game skeletons, long bones cut and splintered with filled ends.
Textures in art/anatomy/tex are original (procedural, make_textures.py).
The hanging bowel link (ClownAnatomy.gut_link) is procedural geometry.
