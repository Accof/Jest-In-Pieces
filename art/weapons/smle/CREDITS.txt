SMLE Mk III rifle model (smle.glb)

This work is based on "Lee Enfield MKIII"
(https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/lee-enfield-mkiii-f6a2160daf134071a84e3cae5f831875)
by Giuliano.Sgroi (https://sketchfab.com/Giuliano.Sgroi)
licensed under CC-BY-4.0 (http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)

Changes made for Jest In Pieces: the bayonet, the sling and the loose display
cartridge were removed; the model was rescaled to 1132 mm and re-oriented; the
bolt was separated into moving parts (Bolt / BoltTurn / CockingPiece) for animation;
sight, muzzle and loading markers were added; textures were downscaled to 2048 px
and re-encoded as JPEG. Build script: source/build_smle_sketchfab.py.

Keep this credit with the game (for example in the in-game credits screen) if it is
ever released.
